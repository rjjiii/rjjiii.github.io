Close a box, sign it, go again

Drag from one dot to a neighbouring dot to draw a line. Close the fourth side of a box and it is yours — write your initials in it and take another turn. Most boxes when the grid is full wins.

Dots and Boxes

☛ Contents

Dots and Boxes is a pen and paper game for two players on a grid of dots. Players take turns drawing lines between the dots, and whoever draws the fourth side of a box signs it with their initials and draws again. When every line has been drawn, the player who signed the most boxes wins.

Rules

  • The board is a square grid of 6 by 6 dots, which makes 25 boxes and 60 lines.
  • Players take turns drawing one line between two dots that are next to each other, across or down. Diagonal lines are not allowed, and a line cannot be drawn twice.
  • A player who draws the fourth side of a box claims it, writes their initials in it, and must draw another line.
  • One line can close two boxes at once. The player claims both.
  • If a player's line closes no box, the turn passes to the other player.
  • The game ends when all 60 lines have been drawn. The player with the most boxes wins. With 25 boxes, the game cannot end in a tie.
