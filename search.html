Settings

Settings are stored on this device and won't transfer to another. Clearing browsing data will clear these settings as well. If you want the custom settings to persist on Apple devices beyond one week, add the web page to your home screen.

(If you save custom settings they will appear here.)
The search form accent color can be anything you choose. It can be a color associated with the website or preferred by a client. If you don't want an accent color, just set it to black (#000000).
This can be anything you choose. It appears above the search bar and in the browser's title bar. It could be the name of the search engine you select below. The default "Finder-Spyder" is simply the fictional search engine used in films and television in place of branded websites like YouTube, Yahoo, or Google.
To get this value:
  1. Go to a website and perform a search for "dog". Just type the three-letter word and search.
  2. Copy the URL from your browser's address bar when the search results load.
  3. Paste that URL into the box above. You may see a lot of random information tacked onto the search URL, but if it contains the word "dog", it should work.
  4. Click Save below.

(Dogs and Spiders were two animals associated with the internet early on for their ability to fetch data and weave webs.)
