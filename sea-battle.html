Sea Battle

☛ Contents

Sea Battle is a game of hidden fleets for two players. Each player sets out a fleet of five ships on a grid the other cannot see, then the two take turns firing shots into each other's waters. The first player to sink every ship in the enemy fleet wins.

Two ways to play are included: Sea Battle, the classic, with one shot per turn, and Salvo, where each player fires one shot for every ship they still have afloat.

Rules

  • Each player has a grid of 10 by 10 squares, with columns lettered A to J and rows numbered 1 to 10.
  • Each fleet has five ships: a Carrier of 5 squares, a Battleship of 4, a Cruiser of 3, a Submarine of 3 and a Destroyer of 2.
  • Each player places their fleet in secret. A ship fills a straight line of squares, across or down, and ships may touch but never overlap.
  • Players take turns firing at the other player's grid by naming a square. The other player says whether the shot is a hit or a miss, and both players mark it.
  • A square can be fired at only once.
  • When every square of a ship has been hit, the ship is sunk and its owner says which ship it was.
  • In Sea Battle, each turn is one shot. In Salvo, each turn is one shot for every ship the firing player still has afloat, so a player fires less as they lose ships.
  • The first player to sink every ship in the other player's fleet wins. A turn only fires at one fleet, so the game cannot end in a draw.
